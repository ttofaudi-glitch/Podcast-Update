# Omaha Morning Brief

_Wednesday, November 18, 2026_

## The short version

Good morning, Warren. It is Wednesday, November 18th. Nvidia's revenue beat forecasts by about $7 billion, it forecast more growth, and it added $150 billion to its buyback. Its shares rose after hours. Before that, the Dow fell 849 points to a three-month low. American jets struck Iranian missile sites on Qeshm Island. And a correction on yesterday's report about China.

## Rates and inflation

Retail sales rose 0.2% in October, as expected. The core group that feeds into economic growth rose 0.6%, above forecasts, a sign consumers are still spending.

Alberto Musalem, president of the St. Louis Federal Reserve Bank, said several rate increases are likely needed by next spring. Futures markets see a quarter-point increase in December as all but certain. The minutes of the October meeting come out tonight, Hong Kong time.

The 10-year Treasury yield eased to 5.10%, and the 2-year to 4.80%. The 3-month bill pays 4.33%. The dollar reached its highest level in about 19 months against a basket of currencies. In Britain, unemployment fell to 4.6%, but pay growth slowed to 3.5%.

## Markets

The Dow fell 849 points, or 1.64%, to 50,759, a three-month low. The S&P 500 fell 1.03%, to 7,341, its lowest close since July, and the Nasdaq fell 0.74%. Selling picked up after reports that a classified network of the National Security Agency had gone down. The agency said it happened during scheduled maintenance and was not a cyberattack.

Brent crude rose 0.8%, to $93.14 a barrel, after Monday's 3.6% fall, as attacks on tankers continued. West Texas Intermediate settled at $86.01. Gold is near $3,850 an ounce, close to its lowest in 13 months.

## Companies

Nvidia's revenue rose 103%, to $115.8 billion, with data centers at $107.6 billion. Adjusted earnings were $2.68 a share, against about $2.48 expected. It forecast about $130 billion next quarter, above estimates and assuming no data center sales to China, but a lower gross margin of 71 to 72%, down from about 75%. It returned $28 billion to shareholders in the quarter. Interim chief executive Colette Kress said the new Vera Rubin chips are off to an explosive start. The shares rose about 2 to 4% after hours.

Home Depot's sales rose 4.9%, above estimates, and it named finance chief Richard McPhail as its next chief executive. But its profit outlook was cautious, and the shares fell about 2.4%. Disney beat estimates and raised its dividend by a third, to $2 a year. Streaming profit more than doubled, to $824 million, and the parks had a record fourth quarter. But the shares fell 1.5%.

## The world

A correction. Yesterday we said Beijing had not confirmed Thursday's meeting of Presidents Xi, Trump and Putin. That was wrong. China's Foreign Ministry confirmed it on Monday and said the leaders will discuss world peace and strategic stability.

American jets struck a Revolutionary Guard missile site on Qeshm Island, after Iranian drones targeted an American radar in Bahrain. Central Command says the drones were stopped without damage. Several more ships hit mines in the Strait of Hormuz.

In Manila, President Trump met the leaders of India, Japan and Australia and announced agreements on critical minerals. He signed a statement with Thailand on a trade deal, with details still to come.

In Israel, the parliament elected Matan Kahana, an ally of Gadi Eisenkot, as speaker, replacing Netanyahu's Likud. Jensen Huang will testify to House committees on December 1st. In Alaska, Mary Peltola has just under 50% of the vote, so the Senate race goes to a ranked-choice count, which early data suggest she would win.

## Coming up

Britain reports October inflation this afternoon, Hong Kong time, expected at 3.6%. Bank Indonesia decides on rates. Target, Lowe's and TJX report before the New York open, and Palo Alto Networks after the close. Anthropic, which makes the system that writes this briefing, is expected to price its share sale today and begin trading Thursday. On Thursday, Walmart reports and the three leaders meet in Shenzhen. That is the briefing. Have a good day.
