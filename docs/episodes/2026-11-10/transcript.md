# Omaha Morning Brief

_Tuesday, November 10, 2026_

## The short version

Good morning, Warren. It is Tuesday, November 10th. Stocks rebounded, and the S&P 500 had its best day since April. Oil fell to about $93 after President Trump said new strikes on Iran are unlikely if talks succeed. Nvidia fell after its board opened an investigation involving its chief executive. China extended its pause on rare earth export controls to January. American consumer prices come out tonight, Hong Kong time.

## Rates and inflation

The 10-year Treasury yield rose to 4.88%, and the 2-year to 4.56%. The 3-month bill pays 4.24%. A $58 billion sale of 3-year notes drew slightly weak demand.

Austan Goolsbee, president of the Chicago Federal Reserve Bank, said he is starting to see inflation rise in services, not only in energy. Futures markets put the odds of a December rate increase at about four in five.

October consumer prices are expected to rise about two to three tenths of a percent, after six tenths in September. The Labor Department will not publish an annual rate this month.

In China, consumer prices rose 0.7% from a year earlier, below forecasts. Producer prices rose 5.7%, the fastest since June 2022, driven by raw materials.

## Markets

The S&P 500 rose 2.35%, to 7,550. The Nasdaq rose 2.74%, and the Dow rose 1,104 points, or 2.15%. Smaller companies did better still. The Philadelphia chip index rose almost 7%, as AMD, Broadcom and Micron recovered part of last week's losses. Energy lagged.

Brent crude fell more than 5%, to about $93 a barrel, its first close below that level since early September. West Texas Intermediate settled at $86.85. President Trump said he had told the military to hold off strikes on Iran's energy plants for five days while talks continue. Gold rose to about $4,140 an ounce.

## Companies

Nvidia fell 4.27%, losing about $190 billion of market value. Its board said independent directors will investigate reports that chief executive Jensen Huang was targeted by a foreign intelligence operation, after he disclosed a personal relationship with a businesswoman American officials reportedly link to China. The board said it has no reason to believe company secrets or national security were compromised. China called the allegation unsubstantiated.

Constellation Energy rose about 10% after adjusted earnings of $5.06 a share, well above estimates. It raised its full-year forecast and bought back $800 million of stock in the quarter. Its nuclear fleet ran at 97% of capacity. NRG Energy agreed to sell six gas plants to LS Power for $4 billion in cash, and will use the money to cut debt and buy back shares. KKR's fee earnings rose 27%, and it added $500 million to its buyback.

After the close, Occidental beat estimates and said it has repaid $10.4 billion of debt this year. Take-Two's bookings fell 18%, but it confirmed that Grand Theft Auto Six launches on November 19th, said preorders are running far above its expectations, and its shares rose after hours.

## The world

China's Commerce Ministry extended its pause on rare earth export controls by two months, to January 10th, along with its pause on a 24% tariff on American goods. That settles the question we raised on Friday.

Iran says it will hit energy targets across the region if its own are struck. Indirect talks continue through Qatar and Pakistan, with no deal announced. More tankers were reported hit near the Strait of Hormuz over the weekend.

Russian, Ukrainian and American delegations are in Abu Dhabi for talks today and tomorrow. In Washington, Stephen Miller started as chief of staff, and Republican Thomas Massie introduced articles of impeachment against Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth. Government funding runs out on December 11th.

## Coming up

Consumer prices come out today at 8:30 am New York time, and CoreWeave reports after the close. The bond market is closed on Wednesday for Veterans Day. On Thursday, Britain reports third-quarter growth, Munich Re reports full results, and Cisco and Applied Materials report after the close. Producer prices follow on Friday. That is the briefing. Have a good day.
