# Omaha Morning Brief

_Monday, November 9, 2026_

## The short version

Good morning, Warren. It is Monday, November 9th. American employers added 165,000 jobs in October, almost three times what was expected. That lifted the odds of a Federal Reserve rate increase in December. The S&P 500 had its worst week since April 2025, as chip stocks kept falling. In Israel, opposition leader Gadi Eisenkot will try to form a government. American consumer prices come out on Tuesday.

## Rates and inflation

The economy added 165,000 jobs in October, against forecasts of about 60,000. Revisions added another 84,000 jobs to August and September. Professional and business services added 70,000 jobs, and health care 42,000. Unemployment fell to 4.1%, and participation edged up to 61.9%. Average hourly pay rose 0.4% in the month and 3.1% from a year earlier. There were soft spots: the broader underemployment rate rose to 7.9%, and the share of people out of work for six months or more is the highest since late 2021.

Futures markets put the odds of a Federal Reserve rate increase in December at about three in four. The 10-year Treasury yield ended the week at 4.86%, and the 2-year at 4.51%. The 3-month bill pays 4.19%.

The University of Michigan's consumer sentiment index rose to 51, a three-month high. Consumers expect prices to rise 4.3% over the next year, down from 4.7%.

## Markets

On Friday the S&P 500 rose 0.31%, to 7,377, and the Nasdaq rose 1.09%. The Dow fell 0.46%. For the week, the S&P 500 fell 2.7%, its worst week since April 2025, and the Nasdaq fell 3.4%, its fourth weekly loss in a row. The Philadelphia chip index fell about 12% for the week, its fifth weekly decline. Nvidia has lost about $1.3 trillion of market value since its October peak.

Brent crude settled Friday at $98.32 a barrel, down from about $113 a week earlier, as traders bet on a deal with Iran. West Texas Intermediate settled at $90.83. Gold is near $4,050 an ounce.

## Companies

Moderna reported a profit when analysts expected a loss, raised its revenue forecast, and its shares rose. Charter lost 233,000 internet customers in the quarter, more than a year earlier, and its shares fell. Humana raised its full-year profit forecast. DraftKings fell more than 26% after cutting its profit outlook to spend heavily on prediction markets. Airbnb rose about 12% and Cloudflare about 13% after their results.

EOG Resources earned more than expected on record production and raised its dividend. Brookfield Asset Management's fee earnings rose 15%.

## The world

Israel's President Herzog gave Gadi Eisenkot the mandate to form a government on Sunday. Parties with 67 of the 120 seats recommended him, against 46 for Prime Minister Netanyahu. He has 28 days, and his path depends on parties that have refused to sit together. Netanyahu has called for a unity government.

President Trump confirmed what he called minor strikes on Iranian air defenses on Qeshm Island, and said diplomacy continues. Iran has rejected the latest American response, and says the Strait of Hormuz stays closed until its conditions are met. There are new reports of tankers hit near the strait.

Stephen Miller replaces Susie Wiles as White House chief of staff, starting today. The Senate races in Alaska and Mississippi are still being counted; Democrats lead narrowly in both.

Russia, Ukraine and the United States are due to meet in Abu Dhabi on Tuesday and Wednesday, their first three-way round since February.

## Coming up

China reports October consumer prices today, and trade figures this week. Its pause on rare earth export controls is due to expire on Tuesday, and Beijing has published no extension. Tuesday brings American consumer prices, expected to rise about 0.3% in October. The Labor Department will not publish an annual rate this month, because last October's prices were never collected during the government shutdown. The bond market is closed Wednesday for Veterans Day. Britain reports third-quarter growth on Thursday, and Cisco and Applied Materials report that evening. Producer prices follow on Friday. That is the briefing. Have a good day.
